# CO2 Footprint privacy policy

Effective 5 October 2026

CO2 Footprint does not collect, store, sell or share any personal or browsing data.

When you click the extension's toolbar button, it reads the load statistics of the page in your current tab (how many bytes each file used). It reads them from your browser's built-in Performance API and calculates an estimate inside your browser. The result is shown to you and then discarded when the popup closes.

- No data leaves your device. The extension makes no network requests.
- Nothing is stored: no history, no cookies, no local storage.
- No analytics, advertising or third-party services are used.
- The extension cannot see any tab you haven't clicked it on.
